## 1. What a proof of RH actually changes

RH says nothing about the difficulty of factoring, and GNFS complexity is untouched.

## 4. Why anyone would recompute this

The certificate is not faster than fixed-base Miller鈥揜abin 鈥?it is ~100脳 slower at 512
bits, and FIPS 186-4 already permits 2鈦宦光伆鈦?error with a handful of rounds. There is no
compliance regime that will swap a fast probabilistic test for a slow deterministic one,
so "sell certificates" is not a business. It is kept in the repo because it is the
cleanest worked example of the one thing a proof of RH genuinely changes: a conditional
constant becomes a statement you can put in a specification.

The uses that survive scrutiny are narrower:

1. **Auditing published zero tables.** The argument-principle check (搂2) does not assume
   RH, so agreement with the on-line count is a real verification of a table someone else
   produced. This is the strongest utility in the repo, and its audience is small.
2. **A cross-check oracle.** 蠄(x) and R(x) each have two independent implementations that
   the pipeline asserts agree; anyone writing 蟺(x) or a sieve can diff against them.
3. **Teaching.** The explicit-formula table (搂3) is the fastest way to make the identity
   concrete, and the SVG charts need no matplotlib.

## 5. What we do not claim

RH gives no factoring speedup, no ECC break, no lattice shortcut. The Cram茅r ceiling at
